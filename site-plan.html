Site Name: Uganda Chamber of Commerce

Site Name

Uganda Chamber of Commerce

This name was selected because it clearly identifies the organization as the official chamber serving Uganda and the surrounding East African region. It conveys authority, local focus, and economic development, making it instantly recognizable to businesses and visitors seeking networking opportunities, event information, and member resources across Uganda and neighboring Kenya.

Site Purpose

The site provides a comprehensive digital hub for the Uganda business community by delivering the following services and information:

  • Chamber membership information, including tier options (Non-Profit, Bronze, Silver, Gold) and associated benefits
  • A searchable business directory allowing visitors to discover local member companies and services
  • Up-to-date listings of chamber events, networking opportunities, and the annual Business Expo
  • Company spotlight features highlighting member achievements and categories
  • Contact information, location details, and social media links for engagement

Scenarios

The following scenarios represent questions asked by target audience members—local business owners, entrepreneurs, and community members interested in the Uganda and Kenya business ecosystem:

  1. What membership benefits and cost tiers are available for a new small business owner, and how do I join the chamber?
  2. How can I find contact information and business categories for specific member companies in the chamber's online directory?

Color Scheme

The following carefully selected colors reflect the professional yet culturally rich identity of Uganda and East Africa:

  • Deep Navy Blue (#0d1b4b): Primary brand color representing stability and trust. Used for page headings, the navigation and footer backgrounds, primary buttons, and key UI elements.
  • Amber Gold (#ffc107): Accent color evoking the historic desert trade. Used for section title underlines, call-to-action buttons, hover states, border accents, and interactive highlights to draw visitor attention.
  • Light Gray (#f4f5f7): Neutral background color. Used for the main page background and section dividers to ensure readability and provide visual breathing room.

Color contrast ratios meet WCAG AA standards for accessibility.

Typography

The following typefaces ensure clear hierarchy and excellent readability across all devices:

  • Inter: The primary typeface used for all headings, body paragraphs, navigation links, and form labels. Its modern, clean design provides superb legibility at both large and small sizes.
  • Open Sans: Used as a robust fallback font if Inter cannot be loaded, and may be used for secondary captions or supporting text. It maintains visual harmony with Inter.
