Identity

Site Name

Jinja City Guide

Chosen because it instantly communicates the location (Jinja, Uganda) and purpose (a helpful guide). Ideal for travelers, ex-pats, and explorers seeking adventure, dining, and culture in East Africa's adventure capital.

Goals

Site Purpose

A local discovery hub for Jinja. Core offerings:

  • Curated adventure listings (rafting, bungee, quad)
  • Restaurant & lodging reviews with prices
  • Event calendar (festivals, marathons, fairs)
  • Travel tips: transport, safety, weather
  • Photo stories from visitor experiences
Audience

Target Users

🌍

Adventure seekers

Backpackers & solo travelers, 22–35, looking for thrill activities and budget tips.

🍽

Family tourists

Parents 30–45 seeking safe dining, kid-friendly spots, and relaxed itineraries.

💼

Local entrepreneurs

Small business owners promoting guesthouses, tours, and cultural experiences.

Research

Scenarios

Questions real users ask:

  1. As a backpacker with two days in Jinja, which rafting companies give the best beginner packages and what is the average cost?
  2. Where can my family eat dinner near the Nile with outdoor seating, vegetarian options, and kid-friendly prices?
  3. What is the best month to visit for dry weather and which cultural festivals are happening this year?
Visuals

Color Scheme

Deep Teal #006d77
River depth & trust
Warm Coral #e29578
Sunsets & energy
Soft Cream #fdf6e3
Background & light

Deep Teal is used for the header, navigation labels, and strong accent elements. Warm Coral highlights buttons, call-to-action links, and selected icon accents. Soft Cream serves as the main page background and card surface color.

Type

Typography

Aa Bb Cc 123

Inter — Primary font for all headings, body text, navigation, and UI. Clean geometric forms with excellent screen legibility. This site plan document uses Inter for headings, labels, and paragraphs.

Open Sans — Fallback and secondary captions. Warm and readable, harmonizes with Inter.
